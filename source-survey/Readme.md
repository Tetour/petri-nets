# Source survey

The survey of the IMS study material that this project started from (31 October 2026). Before any net was drawn, five sweeps went through every relevant file of the course folder `IMS - Modelování a simulace/`: every PDF page was viewed, scans included. Each sweep wrote one report:

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [course-material.md](course-material.md) | lecture slides, MSI notes, scripta, study support, lecture transcripts; the notation conventions |
| [exams-2005-2021.md](exams-2005-2021.md) | final exams 2005–2021: 42 Petri net items (D/D/x marking at time t, M/M/1 variants) |
| [exams-2022-2026.md](exams-2022-2026.md) | final exams 2022/23–2025/26: 20 entries, one of them known only by its file name |
| [midterms.md](midterms.md) | midterms 2010–2026, the Z1–Z24 overview and Hrubý's handwritten solutions |
| [prep-materials.md](prep-materials.md) | student prep compilations (cranycrane, fifinas, luppo, xenix, Bug, …): 63 entries, mostly duplicates, with their nets and errors |

The reports are sorted by **source file**, not by exercise, and quote the task texts verbatim.

## How to use it

- **For one exercise**, read its note instead: the section *Původ a zdroje* gives its origin, source files and pages, the original wording and how existing solutions differ. It is up to date; this survey is not.
- **For one source file** („what is in this PDF?“), this survey is the place: it also lists the files with **no** Petri net exercise, the entries that are unusable, and which tasks are duplicates or variants of each other.

## Caveats

- It is a snapshot from before the exercises were built and is not updated. Its counts are of raw entries, before duplicates were merged into the 118 exercises, and its readings of unclear tasks may differ from the final notes.
- Paths point into the course study folder (outside this repository); some reports give its absolute path on the author's computer.
