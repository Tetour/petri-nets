- **Coffee machine maintenance/idle:**
  - After 5 units idle, maintenance/idle for 20 units: M5 and M10 are essentially the same.
  - After N services, maintenance for 20: M6 (N=50) and M11 (N≈40?) are the same.
  - M7 (sleeps after 5 idle units, wake-up adds 2 units to service) is a close relative.

  - M26 (arrival rate depends on queue ≥ C) is a cousin.
- **Failure (breakdown) family:**
  - M3: failure Exp(200) only cancels the current service.
  - M28: failure Exp(X), repair C, the transaction in service leaves. M24 (Exp(X), duration C) is probably the same task.
  - M29: the failure either destroys the current service or waits for the next service and destroys it.
  - M25: every 10th transaction triggers a failure of duration C.
  - M8: a failure Exp(X) makes the next transaction's service 10 units longer.
  - M18: a failure Exp(X) makes the current service 2 units longer.
- **Service time limit:** M16 (service longer than X → interrupted, transaction leaves) is close to M3/M28, but it is time-based rather than failure-based.
- **"Invent a scenario" pair:** M17 (process priority) and M22 (impatient transaction), both 20 b, both PN + SIMLIB.
